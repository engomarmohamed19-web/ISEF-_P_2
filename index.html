07 / ISEF RESEARCH FRAMEWORK

A question worth
testing carefully.

A testable research proposal with measurable outcomes and honest room for uncertainty. No experimental results or clinical claims are presented.

RESEARCH QUESTION

Can a compact wearable prototype combine optical and motion sensing with a user acknowledgement flow, while meeting defined sensor-error, alert-latency and reliability targets in controlled bench tests?

TESTABLE HYPOTHESIS

Under controlled conditions, the integrated prototype can meet pre-registered engineering thresholds for measurement error, event sensitivity, notification latency and connection reliability. Thresholds must be set before testing.

01Independent variables

Movement condition, sensor placement / fit, connection distance, and event scenario.

02Dependent variables

Sensor error, detection sensitivity, false-alarm rate, notification latency, BLE reliability, battery runtime and NFC success rate.

03Controlled variables

Device revision, sampling settings, reference equipment, test duration, placement protocol and environmental conditions.

Evaluation plan

Limitations and future work ＋

Motion artifacts, fit, skin properties, light leakage and low signal quality can affect optical measurements. 10 / QUICK ANSWERS

Clear answers.
Honest limits.

What the concept does, and what it does not claim to do.

Is MedBand a medical device? ＋

No. It is a student research prototype/concept, not certified, not a diagnostic tool and not a replacement for professional medical care.

Are the displayed readings real? ＋

No. Values such as 78 BPM and SpO₂ 97% are illustrative demo values. No hardware is connected to this website.

Can it detect a fall, fainting or coma? ＋

No reliable detection claim is made. Motion alone cannot establish an event or its cause; any event logic requires validation.

Does NFC share live health readings? ＋

No. NFC is a short-range profile-access path. Avoid storing sensitive health records directly on a tag.

What about GPS and cellular alerts? ＋

GPS and cellular/SIM are optional future extensions, not confirmed built-in hardware. Remote notifications would need an available compatible phone/network path.

11 / GLOSSARY & RESOURCES

A few useful
terms to know.

These links point to manufacturer or standards resources for further research. They are background references, not endorsements or validation of this project.

BLE

Bluetooth Low Energy: a wireless protocol commonly used by low-power devices.

SpO₂

Peripheral oxygen saturation estimate; consumer optical readings need validation and can be affected by use conditions.

NFC

Near Field Communication: very short-range interaction between a phone and a tag.

False-alarm rate

How often a system signals an event when the defined event did not occur.
